About me

Biography

Hello! My name is Jens and I am currently a student in the JavaScript for Web Development course at Högskolan Kristianstad. I grew up in a small border town in northern Sweden in the 90s. As you can imagine, technology was limited. One of my early memories was when my mother got a work laptop that I could play simple games on. I played almost every evening and from there on my interest in computers and technology just exploded!

Academic interest & Hobbies

My interest in this academic field came naturally after years behind the computer screen: playing e-sports, modding and scripting games as well as countless hours spent browsing internet forums etc. I am interested in web development as it combines creativity with technical skills. I like to build and create, both physical and digital. The process of creating something from scratch and see it come to life satisfies me every time. Does not matter if its a web site or an IKEA table! In my free time some of my hobbies include:

  • Football & Sports
  • Travel & Culture
  • Health & Fitness

Future goals

My future goal is to become a Solution Architect with great expertise in web development and other areas. I strive to continuously learn and improve my skills in this field and love to be part of the whole process from idea to finalized product!
